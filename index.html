More ways to work

Online PDF and document tools

Popular PDF converter, OCR and document utility categories in one place.

Convert PDF files

Turn PDF pages into text, Word or Excel files, build a PDF from images, or change image formats between JPG, PNG and WEBP. PDF to Word keeps the text, not the page layout.

Extract text with OCR

Use image to text OCR to read words from JPG, PNG and WEBP pictures. Text-based PDFs give their text directly. Scanned PDFs are read page by page with OCR, in English, up to 20 pages at a time.

Manage PDF documents

Merge, split, compress and rotate PDFs. Add a password with AES-256 or remove one you are allowed to remove. Place a drawn or typed signature on any page.

Frequently asked questions

Is Luvkaam free to use?

Yes. Every tool is free after a one-time registration. Fair-use limits such as the 25 MB file size apply.

Do you upload or store my files?

No. Files are opened and processed inside your browser. They are not sent to Luvkaam. To run some tools your browser downloads open-source helper libraries from the jsDelivr network. The helper libraries do not receive your files.

What do you do with my registration details?

We store your name, email address and phone number to give you access and contact you about your account. If you tick the optional promotional box, we also use them for Luvkaam offers, updates and marketing messages by email, SMS, WhatsApp or phone. You can withdraw that consent at any time from the Privacy page. Read the full Privacy Policy.

How do I withdraw consent or delete my data?

Use the forms on the Privacy page or email hello@luvkaam.com. Promotional consent is withdrawn immediately. Erasure and other requests are handled by our team.

Which files can I use?

PDF files and JPG, PNG, WEBP, GIF or BMP images, up to 25 MB each. Scanned PDF OCR reads the first 20 pages at a time. Password-protected PDFs must be unlocked first.

Can I convert a scanned PDF to Word?

Run "OCR scanned PDF" to get the text, then paste it into "Text to Word & PDF". PDF to Word on its own needs selectable text.

Is a signature added here legally valid?

The Sign PDF tool places an image of your signature on the page. It is not a certificate-based digital signature and does not carry a verified identity. Whether it is accepted depends on the receiver and the type of document.

Do I need to be 18 to register?

Yes. Luvkaam is for people aged 18 and over, and the registration form asks you to confirm this.

Document tips

Learn how to work with your files

Practical guides for common document problems.

How to extract text from an image

Use OCR to recognise printed text in a clear photo. Better lighting, straight alignment and higher resolution improve recognition accuracy.

How to copy text from a PDF

If a PDF has selectable text, extract and copy it. If the pages are scanned images, run OCR first, because the words are not yet text.

Which file format should I pick?

Use JPG for photos, PNG for screenshots and graphics, PDF for sharing with a fixed layout, and Word when you need to edit the text.
